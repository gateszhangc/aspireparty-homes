Message from the Leadership

Leadership with Delivery, Continuity and Trust

Since being elected, our priority has been clear: to make our community work for all. After years of neglect, we restored vital services and took action to support people through the housing crisis and rising living costs.

We believe the council should always be on the side of its community — listening, acting, and supporting residents at every stage of life. Housing has been a top priority. Everyone deserves a decent home, free from overcrowding, and we are committed to building and improving homes that people can truly afford, so families can put down roots and thrive.

Strong public services are the foundation of a fair borough. We are determined to give children and young people the best start in life, to provide all our residents with a good standard of living, and ensure older residents and those with care needs are supported with dignity.

By delivering joined-up services and investing in housing, leisure, waste, youth provision, community spaces, public health, education and community safety, we are building better, stronger neighbourhoods for all.

Manifesto

Our Plan for a Fairer Borough and a Brighter Future

Cost of Living. Homes. Education. Public Services. Safety. Environment. A council that listens.

01. Tackle the Cost of Living Crisis

Support for families, children and pensioners through the cost of living crisis. Freeze Council Tax, establish funds for education, provide free school meals, and create a homelessness fund.

02. Homes for the Future

Housing delivery, tackling overcrowding, and improving standards for residents. Build a minimum of 4,000 social homes for rent, tackle overcrowding, and hold landlords to account.

03. Accelerate Education

Lifelong learning, strong schools, youth support and better opportunities. Booster classes, breakfast clubs, university bursaries, and restoring specialist support for SEND children.

04. Empower Communities & Fight Crime

Stronger communities, safer streets. More police on the streets, CCTV expansion, violence against women and girls initiatives, and drug rehabilitation programmes.

05. Invest in Public Services

Reliable local services, stronger care, better health provision. Reopen One Stop Shop counters, bring outsourced services back in-house, and invest in social care.

06. A Clean and Green Future

Action on climate, waste, parks, pollution, and transport. Expand tree-planting, improve recycling, install EV charging points, and wage war on fly tipping.
